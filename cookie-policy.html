Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 2026. This policy explains what cookies are, how TK Web Solutions uses them, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember user preferences, analyze how the site is performing, and deliver relevant content and advertisements.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Provider
Strictly Necessary Required for core website navigation, security, and basic functionality. TK Web Solutions
Performance & Analytics Measures page speed, user interaction, and visitor counts (e.g., Google Analytics). Google LLC
Advertising & Targeting Used by Google AdSense to show relevant, non-intrusive advertisements to users. Google AdSense

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on select pages of our website. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other sites on the web.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through AboutAds.info.

4. How Can You Control or Disable Cookies?

You have the right to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies by default, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that if you choose to block or disable all cookies, certain features or interactive forms on our site may not function at optimal performance.
