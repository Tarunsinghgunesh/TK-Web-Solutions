The Studio & Founder Relationship

TK Web Solutions ([https://tkwebsolutions.in/](https://tkwebsolutions.in/)) was founded by Tarun Singh with a clear mission: to provide small businesses, schools, coaching centres, and entrepreneurs with affordable, modern, and high-performance digital tools.

Unlike large agencies where work is delegated to multiple layers of intermediaries, Tarun personally handles architecture, UI design, front-end development, search engine optimization, and direct client support.
