Engineered for Privacy & Empathy

Core features architected into the LUNA mobile experience

100% Private Local Storage

Sensitive cycle and personal health records remain securely on your phone using an embedded local Isar database. No unwanted cloud tracking or third-party data broker sharing.

End-to-End Partner Syncing

Secure, optional invitation-based partner synchronization that helps couples stay informed, supportive, and emotionally aligned during every phase.

Smart Cycle Forecasting

Algorithmic prediction models that learn cycle regularity over time, providing accurate window estimates for periods, ovulation, and luteal phases.

Sanctuary & Wellness Log

Holistic symptom tracking, mood logs, hydration reminders, and empathetic guidance tailored to daily hormonal and energy fluctuations.

Zero Ads, Zero Subscriptions

LUNA is provided 100% free as an independent product proof-of-concept by Tarun Singh. No forced upgrade banners, subscription locks, or intrusive ad networks.

Native 60fps Flutter UI

Smooth, custom-crafted micro-animations and intuitive dark/light aesthetics designed for immediate comfort and seamless one-hand usability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details about LUNA's purpose, status, and privacy

Is LUNA free to use?

Yes. LUNA is completely free to download and use with no hidden paid tiers, monthly subscriptions, or locked features.

Who created LUNA?

LUNA is developed by Tarun Singh, founder and lead developer of TK Web Solutions.

How does LUNA protect my data?

All core records are saved directly in local device storage using Isar DB. Cloud sync is only triggered when explicitly enabled for partner sharing.
