🌙 LUNA Mobile App — 100% Offline Sanctuary Policy

LUNA — Women's Health & Sanctuary (developed by Tarun Singh • TK Web Solutions) is architected from the ground up as an offline-first sanctuary. We hold the firm belief that personal menstrual health and emotional well-being data should belong solely to the user.

1.3.1 Local Isar Database Storage

  • On-Device Only: All cycle logs, ovulation predictions, symptom trackers, period histories, and emotional notes are saved exclusively inside your phone's private Isar Database storage.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Your sensitive cycle details are NEVER uploaded to any external cloud database, server, or central storage.
  • Zero Third-Party Broker Sharing: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your health logs to insurance companies, data brokers, or advertisement networks.

1.3.2 Zero Ads & Zero Tracking Pixels

LUNA contains zero third-party advertisement SDKs, zero tracking pixels, and zero behavioral monitoring scripts. The app operates completely ad-free.

1.3.3 Doctor-Ready PDF Export

When you generate a cycle summary PDF to share with your gynecologist or healthcare provider, the document is compiled locally on your device. You retain full control over how and with whom you share the generated report.

1.3.4 Optional Partner Synchronization

If you choose to use the optional Partner Sync feature, the connection operates through a secure, encrypted token exchange. Only the specific cycle status you choose to share is communicated, without exposing your private notes or full historical health database.

5 Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. TK Web Solutions and our third-party partners use cookies for various purposes.

5.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for a better experience.

5.2 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies currently stored on your device
  • Delete specific or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific or all websites
  • Set preferences for when cookies should be allowed

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website and the ads you see may be less relevant to you.
