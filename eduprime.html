School & Institute Case Study

EduPrime Management System

A comprehensive school management platform and Android APK architecture engineered by Tarun Singh to streamline student records, circulars, and parent communication.

Download EduPrime APK Inquire for School

The Challenge & Problem

Many regional schools and coaching institutes in Rajasthan still rely on manual paper circulars, phone calls, and fragmented messaging for student attendance, event notices, and exam schedules. Parents often miss critical updates, and administrative staff spend unnecessary hours handling repetitive inquiries.

The EduPrime Architecture

Tarun Singh designed and developed EduPrime as a lightweight, fast, and mobile-first solution comprising both a centralized web administrative panel and a dedicated native Android application APK.

Centralized Circulars

Instant digital broadcast of notices, holiday updates, and exam timetables directly to parents' mobile phones.

Student Record Directory

Structured database for student profiles, class rosters, and fee status tracking with role-based access.

Faculty & Staff Access

Dedicated tools for teachers to record attendance and communicate daily academic milestones.

Mobile Android App

Fast, lightweight APK package providing parents and students with one-tap access from their home screens.

Measurable Impact

EduPrime eliminates the delay of physical notices, reduces administrative phone inquiries, and provides institutions with an authoritative digital home that enhances trust among parents.
