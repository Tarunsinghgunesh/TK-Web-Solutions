The Journey of Tarun Singh

From writing my first lines of HTML in Bharatpur to founding TK Web Solutions, studying BBA Digital Marketing at Manipal University Jaipur, and building independent digital products.

1. The Beginning 2. TK Web Solutions 3. Mobile Dev 4. BBA Digital Marketing 5. LUNA & Products 6. Today
Self-Taught Foundations 2021 – 2022

Discovering Code & Web Technologies

My interest in technology started from curiosity: how does a single web link connect a person to a complete business system? Without formal developer bootcamps or outside shortcuts, I immersed myself in self-directed learning — mastering HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.

I focused on understanding core fundamentals rather than relying on bloated templates: building clean layouts, learning DOM manipulation, ensuring mobile responsiveness, and testing cross-browser performance from my desk in Bharatpur.

HTML5 & CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript Responsive Design Self-Taught Problem Solving
Entrepreneurship 2022 – 2023

Founding TK Web Solutions in Bharatpur

Seeing that local schools, coaching institutes, and local shops in Bharatpur struggled with expensive, unreliable agencies or had no digital presence at all, I founded TK Web Solutions ([https://tkwebsolutions.in/](https://tkwebsolutions.in/)).

I began building practical, results-driven websites for real clients — such as Achievers Point Coaching, Ek Pahal India NGO, and local retail and E-Mitra centres. I personally handled consultation, UI design, coding, search optimization, and deployment.

TK Web Solutions Founded Client Project Delivery Local Business Digitalization Direct Client Consultation

Expansion 2023 – 2024

Diving Deep into Android & Flutter App Development

As client needs grew beyond static websites, I expanded my technical domain into mobile application development. I learned Flutter & Dart, native Android concepts, state management, and backend integrations with Firebase and Supabase.

This allowed me to develop complete school management applications (such as EduPrime) and utility APKs, delivering native performance and administrative panels to clients.

Flutter & Dart Android App Architecture Firebase & Supabase EduPrime School App

Higher Education 2024 – Present

Pursuing BBA in Digital Marketing at Manipal University Jaipur

To bridge technical software development with real-world commercial strategy, I enrolled in the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) — Digital Marketing program at Manipal University Jaipur (currently pursuing).

Studying marketing strategy, search engine optimization (SEO), consumer behaviour, branding, and business analytics gave me a major advantage: I build software not just for technical elegance, but with a deep understanding of customer acquisition, user psychology, and market growth.

Manipal University Jaipur BBA Digital Marketing Marketing Strategy & SEO Consumer Behaviour

Product Building 2025 – Present

Building Independent Digital Products: LUNA

Alongside client work at TK Web Solutions, I embarked on building independent digital products. I conceived and engineered LUNA — a native menstrual sanctuary and companion mobile application designed with 100% private local storage (Isar DB), cycle forecasting, and partner syncing.

LUNA is provided 100% free with no paywalls or intrusive ads, serving as a live demonstration of high-fidelity mobile engineering, empathetic UI/UX, and privacy-first product design.
