Process

How We Work

10-step professional process — har stage pe clarity aur quality.

01

Discovery

Aapka business, audience, aur goals samajhte hain. Requirements collect karte hain.

02

Planning

Architecture, sitemap, aur content strategy banate hain. Best approach decide karte hain.

03

Proposal

Detailed proposal with timeline, cost, aur deliverables. Aapke approval ke baad aage badhte hain.

04

Agreement

Written contract/ agreement sign karte hain. Payment terms aur scope clear rehta hai.

05

Design

UI/UX design banate hain. Aapke feedback ke baad finalize karte hain.

06

Development

Clean, fast, aur SEO-friendly code likhte hain. Mobile-first approach.

07

Testing

Har device pe test karte hain. Speed, security, aur functionality check karte hain.

08

Deployment

Live server pe deploy karte hain. Domain, SSL, aur hosting setup karte hain.

09

Training

Aapko website manage karna sikhate hain. Basic edits aur updates ka training dete hain.

10

Support
