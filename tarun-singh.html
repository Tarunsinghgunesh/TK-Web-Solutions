My Story

The Journey Behind
TK Web Solutions

Har business ek chhoti si soch se start hota hai — aur meri journey bhi isi simple thought se shuru hui. Main hoon Tarun Singh, Bharatpur, Rajasthan se — ek web developer aur app developer jo local businesses ko digital duniya mein lekar aata hai.

Starting mein sab kuch asaan nahi tha. Coding seekhna, errors solve karna, clients convince karna — sab kuch ek saath karna ek akele insaan ke liye challenging tha. Par determination ne rasta banaya.

Maine notice kiya ki bahut saare local businesses — coaching centers, schools, small shops — achha kaam kar rahe the, par online presence na hone ki wajah se unki growth ruki hui thi. Isi gap ko bharne ke liye TK Web Solutions ka idea aaya.
