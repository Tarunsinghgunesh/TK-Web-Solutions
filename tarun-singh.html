About Me

Who is Tarun Singh?

I'm Tarun Singh — a self-taught web developer and Android app developer from Bharatpur, Rajasthan. I am the founder and sole developer at TK Web Solutions, a professional digital agency I built from scratch starting in 2022–23.

My journey began with YouTube tutorials and online resources. I spent long hours learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Android Studio — making mistakes, fixing them, and growing through every project. The process wasn't easy, but every challenge made me better.

What truly inspired me was watching local businesses in Bharatpur — coaching centers, schools, small shops — miss out on customers because they had no online presence. I realized I could solve this problem, and that became my mission.

"Every local business deserves a strong online presence — regardless of their size or budget."

Since 2023, I have designed and developed websites and Android apps for coaching institutes, schools, businesses, NGOs, and e-mitra centers. I personally handle every project from initial consultation to post-delivery support — ensuring quality and clear communication at every step.
