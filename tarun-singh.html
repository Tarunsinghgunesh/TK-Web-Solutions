A Little About Me

My background, philosophy, and approach to building software

I'm Tarun Singh — a self-taught web and mobile developer, digital marketing student, and the founder of TK Web Solutions ([https://tkwebsolutions.in/](https://tkwebsolutions.in/)).

I started learning to code through self-directed problem solving around 2021–2022. Rather than relying on theoretical bootcamps, I learned by building real projects for real businesses — coaching institutes, schools, retail shops, and non-profits across Bharatpur and Rajasthan.

Currently, I am pursuing my BBA in Digital Marketing at Manipal University Jaipur. This formal education enables me to look at software not just through technical architecture, but through customer acquisition, search visibility, user psychology, and sustainable business fundamentals.
