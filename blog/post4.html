E-Commerce Website Kya Hoti Hai?

E-Commerce website ek online store hota hai jahan customers:

  • Products dekh sakte hain — photos aur details ke saath
  • Compare kar sakte hain — different options
  • Directly order kar sakte hain — WhatsApp ya form se
  • UPI se payment kar sakte hain
  • Location dhundh sakte hain — Google Maps se

E-Commerce Website Ke Zaroori Features

1. Product Catalog — Categories Ke Saath

Saare products properly organize karo. Jaise kapde ki shop ke liye — Men's, Women's, Kids categories. Har product ki photo, description, price — clearly visible. Customer ko dhundhne mein aasaani honi chahiye.

2. WhatsApp Ordering System

India mein simplest aur most popular method. Customer "Order Now" button click kare — WhatsApp pe aapko message aaye with product details. Aap confirm karo, payment lo, delivery karo. No complicated checkout needed.

3. UPI Payment Integration

QR code ya UPI ID se directly payment. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm — sab supported. Safe, fast, free. TK Web Solutions ki e-commerce sites mein yeh already included hai.

4. Google Maps — Location

Agar customers directly shop pe aana chahein — Google Maps se location find karein aasaani se. Shop ka exact pin aur direction — click karo aur Google Maps mein open hoga.

5. Mobile Friendly Design — Must

E-commerce mein 70% shopping mobile se hoti hai. Aapki product photos mobile pe bhi clear dikhni chahiye. Buttons bade hone chahiye. Checkout easy hona chahiye ek haath se bhi.

6. Search Function

Agar bahut products hain — customer seedha search kar sake. "Blue saree" search karo aur matching products aayein — yeh experience professional lagta hai.
