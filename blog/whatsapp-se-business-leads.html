WhatsApp Se Business Leads Kaise Generate Karein 2026

📅 Feb 22, 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 6 min read

India mein WhatsApp sirf messaging app nahi raha — yeh ek powerful business tool ban gaya hai. Ek client ne mujhe bataya ki sirf WhatsApp Business ke status feature se unhe monthly 15-20 new leads milne lage — bina ek rupee ads mein lagaye. Seedha, organic, free. Main Tarun Singh, TK Web Solutions — aaj detail mein batata hoon ki WhatsApp se business leads kaise generate karein India mein 2026 mein.

WhatsApp Business Setup — First Step

Regular WhatsApp se WhatsApp Business switch karo — free hai. Business account mein milta hai:

  • Business name aur logo
  • Business category
  • Working hours display
  • Auto-reply messages
  • Quick replies (saved messages)
  • Product/service catalogue
  • Message labels (New, Pending, Resolved)

Lead Generation Techniques — Practical Ones

Status Marketing

WhatsApp status roz update karo — work photos, offers, tips, testimonials. 200+ contacts hain toh daily 50-100 log status dekh rahe hain. Yeh free advertising hai. Client wala example yahan hi apply hota hai.

Click-to-Chat Links

wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX — yeh link website pe, Instagram bio pe, visiting card pe. One click se directly aapke WhatsApp pe message. TK Web Solutions har website mein yeh button add karta hai by default.

Broadcast Lists

Existing customers ka broadcast list banao. New offer, seasonal discount, reminder — direct unke inbox mein. Groups se alag hai — broadcast mein woh doosron ke messages nahi dekhte.

Catalogue Feature

Products ya services catalogue WhatsApp pe add karo. Customer directly browse kar sake bina website khole. Yeh feature 2024-25 mein India mein bahut popular hua hai.

Common Mistakes Jo Avoid Karo

  • Spam broadcasts — daily 5+ messages bhejna customers ko irritate karta hai
  • Personal account se business — professional nahi lagta
  • Late replies — WhatsApp pe people fast response expect karte hain
  • No auto-reply — raat ko message aaye toh response ka wait nahi karna chahiye

WhatsApp + website combo India ke business ke liye deadliest combination hai. TK Web Solutions websites mein WhatsApp integration by default hoti hai. Digital marketing tips free mein blog pe hain.

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal

WhatsApp Business free hai?

+
Haan, basic WhatsApp Business app free hai. WhatsApp Business API (for large scale) paid hai.

Broadcast list mein kitne contacts add kar sakte hain?

+
256 contacts per broadcast list. Multiple lists bana sakte ho different segments ke liye.

Kya WhatsApp pe ads run kar sakte hain?

+
Haan, Facebook Ads Manager se WhatsApp click-to-chat ads run kar sakte hain. Very effective India mein.

Auto-reply kaise setup karein?

+
WhatsApp Business app > Settings > Business tools > Away message / Greeting message — easy setup.

Kya customers WhatsApp se directly payment kar sakte hain?

+
WhatsApp Pay available hai India mein — UPI based. Limited but growing use.
