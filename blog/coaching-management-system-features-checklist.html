1. Coaching Centers Ko Software Ki Zaroorat Kyun Hoti Hai?

Bharatpur, Kota, Jaipur, aur North India ke tier-2 shehron mein coaching competition bohot tight ho chuka hai. Pehle zamane mein director ek diary ya register mein bacho ki attendance lagate the aur fee ka hisaab rakhte the. Lekin jab students 100 se 500 cross karte hain, toh yeh problems shuru ho jaati hain:

  • Konse student ki is mahine ki fee pending hai, pata nahi chalta.
  • Parents complain karte hain ki bacha ghar se nikla tha lekin coaching nahi pahuncha.
  • Test results declare karne mein 1 hafte ka manual paper checking delay hota hai.

Ek dedicated Coaching Management System (CMS) in sabhi manual headaches ko automate kar deta hai.

Lead & Admission Manager Online inquiry form + status tracking (Demo, Admitted, Dropped) Inquiry miss nahi hoti; 30% admission badhte hain

Study Material Vault Protected PDF notes + un-downloadable video lectures Piracy-free digital coaching experience
Staff & Faculty Payroll Teacher batch schedules + lecture attendance tracking Accurate monthly salary calculation

3. Automated Fee Management Aur GST Receipt System

Fee collection coaching ki lifeline hoti hai. Software se:

  • Due date se 3 din pehle parent ke WhatsApp par polite reminder message chala jata hai.
  • Receipt par institute ka logo, GSTIN, student roll number aur digital signature auto-generate hoti hai. Dekhein humara Online Invoice & Receipt System.

4. Ready-Made SaaS vs Custom Dedicated Institute Portal

Third-party SaaS platforms (jaise Classplus ya generic apps) monthly per-student commission leti hain aur unke app par aapke competitor ke ads bhi dikhte hain. Iske mukable ek Custom White-Label Portal (jaise hum TK Web Solutions Coaching Portal banate hain) aapka apna branded asset hota hai jisme zero monthly cut lagta hai.
