Google My Business Setup Guide — Free Mein Google Pe Aao — Step by Step

Tarun Singh — TK Web Solutions March 20, 2026 8 min padhne mein Bharatpur, Rajasthan

Google My Business (GMB) — aaj Google Business Profile ke naam se jaana jaata hai — yeh bilkul free tool hai jo aapke business ko Google Maps aur Search pe dikhata hai. Yeh Bharatpur ke local businesses ke liye sabse powerful free marketing tool hai.

Jab koi "coaching near me" ya "emitra center bharatpur" search karta hai — Google Maps pe jo 3 businesses dikhte hain upar — woh Google Business Profile wale hain. Top 3 mein aana = roz dozens of new customers.

Google Business Profile Se Kya Milta Hai?

  • Google Maps pe listing — "near me" searches mein dikhna
  • Google Search pe business info panel — phone, address, hours
  • Customer reviews — trust builder
  • Photos showcase
  • Posts — offers, updates share karo
  • Messaging — directly customers se baat karo
  • Insights — kitne log dekh rahe hain, kahan se

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Step 1 — Account Banao

business.google.com pe jaao. Apna Gmail account se login karo. "Manage now" ya "Add your business" click karo.

Step 2 — Business Name Daalo

Bilkul waisa naam daalo jaisa aapke sign board pe hai — "TK Web Solutions" ya "Achievers Point Coaching". Exact match zaroori hai — spelling galat mat karo. Google isko verify karta hai.

Step 3 — Category Select Karo

Yeh bahut important step hai. Google aapko relevant searches mein isi category se dikhata hai:

  • Coaching center → "Tutoring service" ya "Test preparation center"
  • Web designer → "Website designer"
  • School → "Primary school" ya "Private school"
  • E-Mitra → "Government office" ya "Business center"
  • Shop → Specific category (Clothing store, Electronics store etc.)

Step 4 — Address Add Karo

Exact address daalo — building, street, area, city, PIN code. Google ek postcard bhejega — usually 7-10 din mein Bharatpur mein aata hai. Uspe 6 digit code hoga — woh enter karo aur account verify ho jaata hai.

Step 5 — Phone Number aur Website

Business phone number daalo — wohi number jo sab jagah use karte ho (NAP consistency). Agar website hai — URL daalo. Agar nahi hai — yeh first reason hai website banwane ka!

Step 6 — Business Hours Set Karo

Sahi hours set karo — Monday-Saturday, Sunday band. Special hours — holidays pe "Temporarily closed" set kar sakte ho. Galat hours set karne se customers frustrate hote hain.

Step 7 — Photos Add Karo

Photos wale businesses pe 35% zyada website clicks aate hain. Add karo:

  • Cover photo — best quality main business photo
  • Profile photo — logo ya owner ki photo
  • Interior photos — andar kaisa hai
  • Exterior photos — business kaise dikhta hai bahar se
  • Team photos — staff, teachers
  • Products/Services photos

Step 8 — Reviews Collect Karo

Reviews Google Maps ranking mein sabse bada factor hain. Strategy:

  • Happy customers ko personally review link bhejo WhatsApp se
  • Bill ya receipt ke saath QR code print karo review link ka
  • Visit ke baad follow-up message mein review request karo
  • Har review ka reply do — positive aur negative dono
Important: Fake reviews mat karo — Google detect karta hai aur account suspend ho sakta hai. Sirf genuine satisfied customers se maango.

Posts — Regular Activity Zaroori Hai

Har hafte ek post daalo Google Business Profile pe:

  • Naye batch announcement — "New batch starting April 1"
  • Special offers — "Admission pe 10% discount this month"
  • Results celebrate karo — "Our student got JEE rank 450!"
  • Tips share karo — "5 ways to prepare for board exams"

Google Maps Ranking Kaise Improve Karein?

  • Profile 100% complete banao — har field fill karo
  • Reviews zyada collect karo — minimum 10 target karo first month
  • Regularly posts daalo — active profile = better ranking
  • Questions ka jawab do — Q&A section mein
  • Website link add karo — website SEO se ranking improve hoti hai
  • NAP consistency — naam, address, phone everywhere same

Website + Google Business = Double Visibility

Google Business Profile aapko Maps pe dikhata hai. Website aapko Google Search pe dikhati hai. Dono saath kaam karein — double visibility, double leads.
