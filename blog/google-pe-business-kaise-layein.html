India mein roz 600 million+ Google searches hoti hain. Unme se aapke business se related kitni? Aur aap unhe capture kar rahe ho? Agar nahi, toh yeh guide exactly aapke liye hai. Google Pe Dikh Ne Ke 4 Main Channels

1. Google Organic Search (SEO)

Website ke through Google search results mein dikhna. Free, long-term, high credibility. Time lagta hai — 3-6 months — lekin ek baar ranking aaye toh stable hoti hai. SEO basics guide yahan hai.

2. Google Maps (Local SEO)

Google My Business ke through Maps pe dikhna. "Near me" searches ke liye most effective. Free setup, results 2-4 hafte mein. GMB guide yahan padho.

3. Google Ads (Paid)

Paid placement — immediate visibility. Search pe top mein "Sponsored" ke saath dikhte ho. ₹200/day se start possible. Fast results, but requires budget.

4. Google Shopping (E-Commerce)

Product-based businesses ke liye. Product photos Google search pe dikhte hain. India mein rapidly growing channel.

Step 1 Se Shuru Karo — Today

Website Banwao (Most Important)

Bina website ke Google pe rank karna limited hai. GMB bhi website link ke saath better perform karta hai. TK Web Solutions se ₹5,000 mein shuru karo.

GMB Setup Karo (Second Priority)

Free hai, setup 1 ghante mein. Local searches ke liye game-changer. Verification 5-7 din.

Basic On-Page SEO

Page titles mein location include karo. About page pe "We serve Bharatpur" mention karo. Contact page pe full address, phone, map.

Common Mistakes Jo Sab Karte Hain

  • GMB setup kiya lekin photos nahi daale
  • Website hai lekin mobile pe slow hai
  • Same phone number GMB aur website pe alag-alag hai
  • Reviews milne ke baad respond nahi karte
  • Business description vague — "We provide all services" — helpless hai

Google pe visibility ek process hai — overnight nahi hota. Lekin sahi direction mein ek-ek step uthane se 3-6 months mein significant results aate hain. ❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal

Google pe dikh ne ke liye website zaroori hai?

+
Highly recommended. GMB akele kuch hadd tak kaam karta hai lekin website ke saath results 3x better.

Google Ads vs SEO — kaunsa choose karein?

+
Dono alag purposes ke liye. Ads = immediate results, SEO = long-term free traffic. Ideally dono together.

Google pe negative reviews aaye toh kya karein?

+
Professionally respond karo. Problem resolve karo. Fake reviews report karo Google ko.

Business ka exact naam GMB mein banana chahiye?

+
Haan, exact business naam — keywords stuffing nahi karo. Google penalize kar sakta hai.

Google pe dikhne ke liye paid verification karna padta hai?

+
Nahi. GMB completely free hai. Koi bhi paid verification service genuine nahi hai — scam hai.
