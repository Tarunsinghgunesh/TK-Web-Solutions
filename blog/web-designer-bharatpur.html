Achha Web Designer Kya Karta Hai?

Sirf Design Nahi — Business Goals Samajhna

Ek achha web designer pehle poochta hai — "aapka business kya hai, customers kaun hain, aap website se kya chahte ho?" Sirf sundar dikhne wali website nahi — conversion-focused website banana.

Mobile-First Approach

India mein 78% users mobile se browse karte hain. Jo designer mobile se shuru nahi karta, woh outdated hai. TK Web Solutions har website mobile-first design karta hai.

SEO Foundation

Beautiful website jo Google pe rank na kare — waste of money. Proper headings, meta tags, speed optimization — yeh sab design ke saath hi aana chahiye, baad mein add-on nahi.
