Local SEO Regular SEO Se Alag Kaise Hai?

Regular SEO broad audience target karta hai. Local SEO specific geographic area ke searches ke liye optimize karta hai. "Best coaching center" vs "Best coaching center Bharatpur" — dono alag searches hain aur dono mein alag strategy kaam karti hai. Local businesses ke liye Local SEO zyada valuable hai kyunki intent clear hota hai — woh local customer hai jo aapke paas aa sakta hai.

Local SEO Ke 5 Pillars

1. Google My Business Optimization

GMB sabse important signal hai. Complete profile, correct NAP (Name, Address, Phone), categories, photos, posts — sab matters. GMB guide yahan detail mein hai.

2. Local Keywords

Sirf "coaching center" nahi — "coaching center Bharatpur", "coaching classes Rajasthan", "IIT coaching near me Bharatpur". Location + service = local keyword. Inhe website content mein naturally include karo.

3. Online Reviews

Google reviews ranking mein direct signal hain. 4.5+ rating aur 20+ reviews wali listing consistently top 3 mein rahti hai. Reviews collect karna ek system banana padta hai — not just asking one time.

4. Local Citations

Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, local directories — inpe business information consistent honi chahiye. NAP (Name, Address, Phone) same everywhere — inconsistency ranking hurt karta hai.

5. Location-Specific Website Content

"We serve Bharatpur, Rajasthan" — explicitly mention karo website pe. Location page banana helpful hai agar multiple areas cover karte ho.

Kitne Time Mein Results Milenge?

GMB optimize karne ke 2-4 hafte mein improvement shuru hoti hai. Full results typically 2-3 months mein. Reviews gather karte rehne se position aur strong hoti jaati hai. Yeh ek ongoing process hai, one-time fix nahi.

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal

Local SEO ke liye website zaroori hai?

+
Highly recommended. GMB akele bhi kaam karta hai lekin website + GMB combo mein results significantly better hain.

Google Maps pe kaise ranking hoti hai?

+
GMB completeness, reviews, distance, relevance — yeh teeno main factors hain Google Maps ranking ke.

Local SEO free hai ya paid?

+
Mostly free — GMB, citations, website optimization. Paid ads optional hain for faster results.

+
Har branch ka separate GMB listing banao — same account se manage kar sakte ho. Website pe location pages banao.

+
After service satisfaction check karo, phir politely review request karo with direct Google link. WhatsApp se easy hai.
