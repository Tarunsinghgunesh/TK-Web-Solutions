Domain Aur Hosting Kya Hota Hai — Beginners Guide 2026

📅 Jan 10, 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 5 min read

Website banwane ki baat hoti hai toh do words baar baar aate hain — domain aur hosting. Aur zyaadatar log yahan confuse ho jaate hain. Toh chaliye seedha example se samajhte hain: Agar aapka business ek ghar hai, toh domain us ghar ka address hai (jaise "tkwebsolutions.in") aur hosting woh zameen hai jis pe ghar bana hua hai. Dono alag-alag cheezein hain, dono zaroori hain.

Domain Name — Aapka Digital Address

Domain woh naam hota hai jo browser mein type karte ho. Jaise "google.com" ya "tkwebsolutions.in". Yeh ek baar khareedna padta hai aur annually renew hota hai.

Konsa Domain Extension Choose Karein?

  • .in — Indian businesses ke liye best. Local SEO mein bhi faayda. Cost: ₹500-700/year.
  • .com — Global recognition, slightly expensive. ₹800-1,200/year.
  • .co.in — Indian companies ke liye. ₹400-600/year.
  • .org — NGOs aur organizations ke liye.

Meri recommendation — Bharatpur ke local businesses ke liye .in domain best hai. Local SEO mein help karta hai aur affordable bhi hai. Name short rakho, business naam include karo, numbers avoid karo.

Web Hosting — Aapki Website Kahan Rahegi

Hosting woh computer (server) hai jahan aapki website ki files store rehti hain. Jab koi aapki website open karta hai, uska browser hosting server se data laata hai. Hosting ke bina website exist nahi kar sakti.

Hosting Ke Types

  • Shared Hosting: Entry-level. ₹1,500-3,000/year. Small business websites ke liye perfect.
  • VPS Hosting: Better performance. ₹5,000-15,000/year. Growing businesses ke liye.
  • Cloud Hosting: Scalable, fast. ₹3,000-10,000/year. E-commerce ke liye good.

Domain + Hosting Kahan Se Khareedein?

India mein popular options hain: Hostinger (sabse affordable), GoDaddy India, BigRock, Bluehost India. Main personally Hostinger recommend karta hoon — ₹99/month se shuru hota hai basic plan, aur performance bhi achhi hai.

Ek important tip — domain aur hosting alag-alag jagah se kharidne ki zaroorat nahi. Same provider se lo, management easy hogi. ❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal

Domain aur hosting ek hi jagah se lena chahiye?

+
Zaroor nahi, lekin same provider se lena easy hota hai. Management ek jagah, renewal ek jagah.

.in domain better hai ya .com?

+
Indian local businesses ke liye .in better hai — SEO mein faayda aur cost bhi kam. .com global recognition ke liye.

Free hosting use kar sakte hain?

+
Technically haan, practically nahi. Free hosting mein ads aate hain, slow hoti hai, aur aapka subdomain hoga — professional nahi lagta.

Domain naam change ho sakta hai baad mein?

+
Nahi, domain naam change nahi hota. Isliye soch-samajh ke naam choose karo. Business name ya location include karo.

Hosting renew nahi kiya toh kya hoga?

+
Website down ho jaayegi. Important hai renewal dates yaad rakhna. Zyaadatar providers 15-30 din pehle reminder bhejte hain.
