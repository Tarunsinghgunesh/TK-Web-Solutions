Online Admission Form Ke Real Fayde

  • 24/7 availability: Parents busy time pe bhi form fill kar saken
  • Lead capture automation: Har submission WhatsApp/email pe notify
  • Professional image: Well-designed form = trustworthy coaching impression
  • Data organized: Sab inquiries ek jagah — follow-up easy
  • Conversion higher: Form wali website pe 3x zyada inquiries convert hoti hain

Admission Form Mein Kya Fields Honi Chahiye?

Required Fields (Keep It Short)

Long forms = low submissions. Sirf zaroori fields rakho pehle:

  • Student name
  • Parent name
  • Class / Course
  • WhatsApp number
  • Preferred batch (morning/evening)

Optional Fields

Email, previous school, board — yeh baad mein pooch sakte ho jab call/meeting hogi.

Conversion Tip

Form ke saath ek clear value proposition likho: "Fill karo, 24 ghante mein call milega." Specific promise = higher trust = more submissions.

WhatsApp Integration — Instant Notifications

Best part — jab bhi form fill ho, turant aapke WhatsApp pe message aaye. TK Web Solutions yeh feature include karta hai. Iska matlab — no inquiry missed, fast follow-up, better conversion.
