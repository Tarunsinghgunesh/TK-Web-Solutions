WhatsApp Business App vs WhatsApp Cloud API 2026: Green Tick & Chatbots Guide

1. WhatsApp Marketing Ka Reality Check

Bharat mein email ka open rate mushkil se 15% hota hai, jabki WhatsApp messages ka open rate 98% se zyada hai. Har coaching director, shopkeeper aur business owner customer se WhatsApp par hi baat karta hai. Lekin personal number ya free app se hazaaron logo ko broadcast bhejne par number permanent block ho jaata hai.

2. WhatsApp Business App (Free) Ke Limits Aur Nuksaan

Play Store se download hone wali free WhatsApp Business App chhote dukaandaron ke liye theek hai jahan roz 5-10 inquiries aati hain. Lekin grow hote business ke liye isme badi bandishein hain:

  • Broadcast list sirf unhi logo ko deliver hoti hai jinhone aapka number phone book mein save kiya ho.
  • Ek waqt par sirf ek ya do devices par active chal sakti hai.
  • Advanced chatbot (jaise buttons, dynamic fee receipt, auto-lead routing) nahi lag sakte.

3. WhatsApp Cloud API Kya Hai Aur Kaise Kaam Karti Hai?

Meta ne developers ke liye direct WhatsApp Cloud API launch kiya hai. Iska koi phone screen nahi hota; yeh code aur software (CRM) se connect hoti hai. Website par jab koi lead aati hai, toh API automatically customer ko personalized welcome message bhej deti hai bina kisi insaan ke mobile chhue.

4. Full Comparison Table: App vs Cloud API

Feature WhatsApp Business App WhatsApp Cloud API
Target Users Small single-owner shops Growing businesses, coaching institutes, schools
Number of Agents 1 phone + 4 linked browsers Unlimited team members simultaneous login
Broadcast Delivery Only if contact is saved 100% delivered to all contacts
Account Ban Risk High (if reported by 5 users) Zero (Official Meta compliant templates)
Interactive Buttons No clickable quick-reply buttons Rich interactive buttons & list menus
Green Tick Badge Not eligible Eligible for official Meta Green Badge

5. Green Tick Verification Rules

Green Tick paane ke liye Meta aapka Facebook Business Manager verify karta hai. Iske liye GST certificate, electricity bill, working website with domain email (jaise contact@tkwebsolutions.in), aur 3-4 reputed news articles (PR) ka zikr hona zaroori hota hai.
