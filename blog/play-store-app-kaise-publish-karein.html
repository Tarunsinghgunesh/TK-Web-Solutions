Play Store Pe App Kaise Publish Karein — 2026 Guide

📅 Mar 3, 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 7 min read

App ban gayi — exciting moment! Lekin Play Store pe publish karna last aur critical step hai. Ek-do common mistakes se app reject ho jaati hai — aur resubmit karne mein time waste hota hai. Main Tarun Singh, TK Web Solutions — personally 20+ apps publish kar chuka hoon Google Play pe — aaj exact step-by-step guide deta hoon ki Play Store pe app kaise publish karein bina rejection ke.

Prerequisites — Pehle Yeh Ready Karo

  • Completed, tested Android app (.aab file — App Bundle format)
  • Google Developer Account (play.google.com/console)
  • ₹1,750 one-time registration fee (credit/debit card ya UPI)
  • App icon (512x512 PNG)
  • Feature graphic (1024x500 PNG)
  • Screenshots — minimum 2, maximum 8 (phone)
  • Privacy Policy URL (ab mandatory hai)

Step-by-Step Submission Process

Step 1: Developer Account

play.google.com/console pe jaao. Google account se sign in karo. Registration form fill karo — name, address, phone. ₹1,750 pay karo. 24-48 hours mein account activate.

Step 2: New App Create Karo

"Create App" button. App name, default language, free/paid select karo. App type — app ya game.

Step 3: Store Listing

App ka title (50 chars max), short description (80 chars), long description (4000 chars). Keywords naturally include karo. Screenshots upload karo. Icon aur feature graphic upload karo. Category select karo.

Step 4: Privacy Policy

Ab mandatory hai — ek simple privacy policy webpage banana padega. TK Web Solutions yeh help karta hai — basic privacy policy template provide karta hai.

Step 5: Content Rating

App ko content rating milna zaroori hai. Questionnaire fill karo honestly — galat answer pe ban ho sakta hai.

Step 6: Release

.aab file upload karo. Internal testing → Closed testing → Open testing → Production — yeh stages hain. First time direct production release possible hai. Review 1-7 din. Published!

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal

Play Store developer account ek baar ka kharcha hai?

+
Haan, ₹1,750 one-time fee. Uske baad unlimited apps publish kar sakte ho.

App review mein kitna time lagta hai?

+
Typically 1-3 din. Kuch cases mein 7 din tak. Pehli app thoda zyada time le sakti hai.

App reject ho jaaye toh kya karein?

+
Rejection email mein reason hota hai. Fix karo, resubmit karo. Common reasons: policy violation, incomplete privacy policy.

Free app better hai ya paid?

+
India mein free app + in-app purchases model best hai. Paid apps ki downloads kam hoti hain.

Ek account se kitni apps publish kar sakte hain?

+
Unlimited. Ek developer account se multiple apps manage kar sakte ho.
