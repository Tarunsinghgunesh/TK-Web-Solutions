Mobile App Se Paise Kaise Kamayein: AdMob, Subscriptions Aur In-App Purchases

📅 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 9 min read

⚡ Quick Takeaway / Summary

Ek mobile app se paise kamane ke 5 primary models hote hain: Google AdMob advertisements, monthly/yearly subscription plans, one-time in-app purchases (notes, test series), affiliate marketing, aur brand sponsorships. Ek achhi 10,000 active users wali utility ya coaching app mahine ka ₹30,000 se ₹1,50,000 generate kar sakti hai.

1. App Monetization Ka Asli Math: Free App Se Paise Kaise Aate Hain?

Play Store par 96% se zyada apps bilkul free download hoti hain. Fir bhi Instagram, Truecaller, Canva, ya local coaching apps crorepati business ban chuki hain. Iska secret yeh hai ki app download hone ke baad monetize hoti hai.

Agar aap coaching institute chala rahe hain, education portal bana rahe hain, ya koi daily utility tool bana rahe hain, toh sahi monetization strategy shuruat se planned honi chahiye.

2. Model 1: Google AdMob (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Ads)

Google AdMob Google ka official mobile ad network hai (jaise websites ke liye Google AdSense hota hai):

  • Banner Ads: Screen ke sabse neeche ya upar chhota ad strip jo hamesha rehta hai.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full screen ad jo do natural transitions ke beech aata hai (jaise ek chapter khatam hone par).
  • Rewarded Video Ads: User ko reward milta hai ad dekhne par (jaise video ad dekhne par 1 free mock test unlock hona). Iska eCPM sabse high hota hai!

3. Model 2: Subscriptions (Monthly / Yearly Pass)

Recurring Revenue business ka sabse solid model hai. Example: Coaching app mein student ko free recorded video milte hain, lekin live classes aur teacher doubt session ke liye ₹499/month ka subscription plan hota hai.

4. Model 3: In-App Purchases (Digital Notes, Test Series)

Agar aap Indian market mein kaam kar rahe hain, toh UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) integration ke through digital content sell karna sabse profitable model hai:

  • PDF study notes: ₹49 – ₹199
  • Exam mock test bundle: ₹299 – ₹599
  • Certificate download fee: ₹99

5. Monetization Models Ka Realistic Earnings Comparison Table

Monetization Model Required User Base Average Monthly Earnings (India) Best Suited For
Google AdMob High (> 25,000 Daily Active Users) ₹15,000 – ₹60,000 (Low eCPM in India) Free utilities, games, wallpaper apps
In-App Purchases (UPI) Moderate (1,000 – 5,000 Users) ₹40,000 – ₹1,50,000+ Coaching institutes, colleges, skill trainers
Subscription Pass Engaged (500 – 2,000 Regular Users) ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000+ SaaS tools, daily test portals, premium clubs
Local Sponsorships Local (2,000 – 5,000 City Users) ₹20,000 – ₹50,000 direct sponsor City directories, news apps, school portals

💡 Indian Market Ke Liye Golden Rule:

India mein log ads bardasht kar lete hain lekin debit card se automatic monthly auto-debit nahi pasand karte. Isliye One-time UPI Payment via Razorpay sabse zyada convert hota hai!

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQs)

AdMob par 1000 views par kitne rupaye milte hain?

+
India mein AdMob banner ads ka eCPM lagbhag ₹10 se ₹40 hota hai, jabki rewarded video ads par ₹80 se ₹250 tak mil sakta hai.

Kya app mein payment lene ke liye GST zaroori hai?

+
Shuruat mein individual savings account ya proprietor PAN card par Razorpay test mode aur basic KYC activate ho jaata hai. Turnover badhne par GST mandatory hota hai.

Kya Google Play Store in-app purchases par commission leta hai?

+
Google Play Billing digital goods par 15% service fee leta hai. Lekin offline services ya physical products par direct Indian UPI gateways (0-2% fee) use kiye ja sakte hain.
