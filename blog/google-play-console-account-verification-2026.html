Google Play Console Account Verification 2026: 20 Testers Aur D-U-N-S Niyam

📅 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 11 min read

⚡ Quick Takeaway / Summary

Organization accounts ke liye D-U-N-S number aur official company verification mandatory hai.

1. 2026 Play Console Policy Changes: Kyun Hua Itna Strict?

Pehle zamane mein koi bhi developer $25 dekar Google Play Console account kholta tha aur agle din apni APK upload karke 4 ghante mein live kar deta tha. Iska nuksaan yeh hua ki Play Store par lakhon crash hone wali, malware aur fake clone apps bhar gayi.

Google ne in sabhi problems ko khatam karne ke liye strict rules banaye hain. Agar aap coaching institute, school ya startup ke liye app launch kar rahe hain, toh in rules ko samjhe bina app submit karenge toh account suspend hone ka khatra rehta hai.

2. Personal Account vs Organization Account: Konsa Banayein?

3. 20 Closed Testers for 14 Days: Step-by-Step Rule

Agar aapne 13 November 2023 ke baad Personal Play Console account banaya hai, toh Google aapse production access tab tak block rakhta hai jab tak aap yeh conditions poori na karein:

  1. App ka first draft Closed Testing Track mein upload karein.
  2. Kam se kam 20 real users ke email addresses (Google Group ya email list) add karein.
  3. Un 20 users ko Play Store opt-in link bhej kar app download karwayein.
  4. Sabhi 20 users ke phone mein app kam se kam 14 lagataar din (consecutive days) tak installed rehni chahiye.
  5. 14 din poore hone ke baad Console par "Apply for Production" button unlock hota hai, jisme Google aapke testing feedback ke sawaal poochta hai.

4. D-U-N-S Number Kya Hai Aur Free Kaise Banwayein?

D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System) ek 9-digit global business identification number hai jo Dun & Bradstreet (D&B) provide karta hai. Agar aap Organization account bana rahe hain:

  • dnb.com par jakar check karein ki kya aapki company pehle se listed hai.
  • Agar listed nahi hai, toh Google Play Console verification portal ke through free application submit karein.
  • Aapko GST certificate, Udyam Registration (MSME), ya PAN card upload karna hota hai. 1 se 2 hafte mein D-U-N-S number generate ho jaata hai bilkul free.

5. App Submission Checklist

📋 Submission Se Pehle Yeh 4 Cheezein Ready Rakhein:

1. Privacy Policy URL: Ek live, working URL hona chahiye jaise TK Web Solutions Privacy Policy.
2. Data Safety Form: Declare karein ki aap user ka phone, location ya storage data lete hain ya nahi.
3. App Icon & Feature Graphic: 512x512 PNG icon aur 1024x500 banner.
4. Phone Screenshots: Minimum 4 high-resolution mobile screenshots.

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQs)

Google Play Console account registration fee kitni hai?

+
Google Play Console ki one-time lifetime fee $25 USD (lagbhag ₹2,100 se ₹2,200 INR) hoti hai, jo international credit card se pay hoti hai.

Kya 20 testers automated bots ho sakte hain?

+
Kadaapi nahi! Googlebot IP address aur device activity track karta hai. Fake ya bot accounts use karne par Play Console account permanent ban ho sakta hai.

Application review hone mein kitna time lagta hai?

+
14 din ki closed testing ke baad production review mein aam taur par 2 se 7 working days lagte hain.
