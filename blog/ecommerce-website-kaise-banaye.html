E-Commerce Website Kaise Banaye — India Guide 2026

E-Commerce Website Ke Essential Features

Pehle samajhte hain ki minimum kya chahiye ek working online store ke liye:

  • Product catalogue: Photos, description, price clearly
  • Shopping cart: Multiple items ek saath
  • Payment gateway: Razorpay, Paytm, UPI accept karne ke liye
  • Order management: Orders track karne ke liye
  • Mobile-friendly: 70% India ke shoppers mobile se karte hain
  • SSL certificate: "https" — payment security ke liye zaroori

Konsa Platform Choose Karein?

WordPress + WooCommerce

Best for: Small to medium stores. Cost-effective. Highly customizable. TK Web Solutions primarily isi pe kaam karta hai. ₹15,000-30,000 mein complete store ready.

Shopify

Easy to use. International reach. Monthly charges ($29+/month) — long-term expensive. Agar global sell karna hai toh consider karo.

Custom Development

Specific requirements ke liye. Expensive (₹50,000+). Generally small businesses ke liye over-kill hai start mein.

My Recommendation

WordPress + WooCommerce + Razorpay — yeh combination India ke small businesses ke liye sabse cost-effective aur functional hai. TK Web Solutions yahi banata hai.

E-Commerce Success Ke Liye Tips

  • Product photos professional quality mein lo — white background, multiple angles
  • Delivery policy clear likho — speed aur cost
  • Return policy mention karo — trust badhta hai
  • Customer reviews add karo products pe
  • WhatsApp order option bhi rakho — many Indian buyers prefer karte hain

E-commerce website ek serious investment hai — lekin agar products hain toh returns bahut better hain vs Amazon commission. TK Web Solutions complete e-commerce solution deta hai — design se lekar payment integration aur launch tak. Aur e-commerce tips blog pe milenge.
