Website Launch Ke Baad Kya Karein — Post-Launch Checklist

📅 Mar 18, 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 6 min read
📖 Chapter Tools & Share:
💬 WhatsApp 💼 LinkedIn 🐦 X / Twitter

Website launch ho gayi — WhatsApp status laga diya, kuch congratulations aaye, aur phir... traffic zero. Ek hafte baad bhi koi visitor nahi. Kya galat hua? Galat kuch nahi hua — sirf ek baat miss hui: website launch sirf pehla step hai, asli kaam baad mein hota hai. Main Tarun Singh, TK Web Solutions — yeh 10-step post-launch checklist har Indian business owner ke liye zaroori hai.

Step 1-3: Technical Setup (Day 1)

Google Search Console

search.google.com/search-console pe jaao. Website add karo. Sitemap submit karo (yoursite.com/sitemap.xml). Yeh Google ko signal karta hai ki "meri website exist karti hai" — indexing fast hoti hai.

Google Analytics

analytics.google.com pe account banao. Tracking code website mein add karo. Ab se pata chalega ki kitne log aa rahe hain, kahan se aa rahe hain, kaun sa page popular hai.

Google My Business Update

GMB listing mein nai website ka URL add karo. Photos update karo. Timing verify karo. GMB guide yahan hai.

Step 4-7: Promotion (Week 1)

WhatsApp Blast

Apne sare contacts ko website ka link bhejo — personal message, group nahi. "Hamari nai website launch hui hai, ek baar zaroor dekho" — simple aur effective.

Social Media Announce

Facebook, Instagram pe announcement post karo. Short video ya screenshot ke saath. Local groups mein share karo — Bharatpur groups mein bahut engagement hoti hai.

Existing Customers Ko Batao

Physical shop hai toh visiting card pe website URL add karo. Receipts pe print karo. Customers ko directly batao.

Google Pe Manual Listing Check Karo

"Site:yourwebsite.in" Google pe search karo — agar pages index ho rahe hain toh dikh jaayenge. Agar nahi toh Search Console mein check karo.

Step 8-10: Long-term Growth

  • Monthly blog post: Fresh content = better SEO ranking
  • Reviews collect karo: Happy customers se Google reviews maango
  • Local directories: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART pe bhi listing karo

Website ek asset hai jo time ke saath zyada valuable hota hai — agar sahi se maintain karo. TK Web Solutions post-launch support bhi deta hai. Aur growth tips ke liye regularly blog check karo.

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal

Website launch ke kitne time baad Google pe dikh aata hai?

+
Search Console sitemap submit ke 1-4 hafte mein. Domain age bhi matter karta hai.

Google Analytics free hai?

+
Haan, bilkul free. Basic analytics ke liye GA4 (Google Analytics 4) enough hai.

Kya sirf WhatsApp se website promote ho sakti hai?

+
Haan, local audience ke liye WhatsApp sabse effective hai India mein. Seedha log tak pahunchta hai.

Website pe content regularly update karna zaroori hai?

+
Haan, Google fresh content ko prefer karta hai. Monthly ek update bhi helpful hai.

Visiting card pe kaunsi website info daalni chahiye?

+
URL, QR code, aur WhatsApp number — teeno ka combination best hai.
