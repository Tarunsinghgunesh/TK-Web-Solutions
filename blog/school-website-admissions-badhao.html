Parents Aaj Kaise School Dhundh Rahe Hain?

Forget word-of-mouth as the primary channel — woh ab secondary hai. India mein 2026 mein parents ka typical journey: Google search → website visit → reviews padhna → call ya visit. Agar step 2 (website) missing hai, toh 70% parents aage proceed nahi karte. Yeh harsh reality hai.

School Website Mein Kya Hona Chahiye?

Parents Jo Cheezein Sabse Pehle Dekhte Hain

  • Fee structure: Clear aur transparent — hidden fees doubt create karte hain
  • Admission process: Step-by-step — form, documents, dates
  • Results aur achievements: Board results, competitions — proof of quality
  • Infrastructure photos: Classrooms, labs, playground — parents visualize karte hain
  • Faculty profiles: Qualifications aur experience — trust builder
  • Contact aur location: Phone, address, Google Maps embed

Online Admission Form — Game Changer

Raat 11 baje bhi parent admission form fill kar sake — yeh convenience massive advantage deta hai. Form submission pe immediately WhatsApp notification milna — yeh professional touch hai jo small schools ko bade schools jaisa feel deta hai.

Local SEO — "School Near Me" Pe Dikhna

Google My Business pe school list karo. "CBSE school Bharatpur", "English medium school near me" — yeh searches daily hoti hain. Website + GMB + reviews = top 3 position possible.
