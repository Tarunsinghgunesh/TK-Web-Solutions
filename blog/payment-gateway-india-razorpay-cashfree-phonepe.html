Best Payment Gateways in India 2026: Razorpay, Cashfree & PhonePe PG Compared

📅 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 10 min read

⚡ Quick Takeaway / Summary

Payment Gateway aapki website ya app par customers se UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Card, aur Netbanking se direct bank account mein paise collect karne ka bridge hai. Razorpay sabse developer-friendly aur reliable hai, Cashfree payouts ke liye best hai, aur PhonePe PG 0% fee promo ke saath emerging option hai.

1. Payment Gateway Kya Hai Aur Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Pehle zamane mein agar aapko online koi product ya course bechna hota tha, toh aap customer ko apna QR code ya bank account number bhejte the, fir woh screenshot bhejta tha aur aap 1 ghanta bank statement check karte the. Yeh process scale nahi ho sakta.

Payment Gateway (PG) ek automated digital cashier hota hai. Jab customer "Pay Now" par click karta hai, toh popup mein UPI, Card ya Netbanking options aate hain. Payment complete hote hi webhook trigger hota hai, invoice generate hoti hai, aur access unlock ho jata hai — 100% automated bina kisi manual intervention ke!

3. Transaction Fees (TDR / MDR) Aur Settlement Cycles (T+1 vs T+2)

India mein RBI ke niyam anusar:

  • UPI & RuPay Debit Card: Zero MDR transactions supported by government policy for verified small merchants.
  • Credit Card & Netbanking: 2% + 18% GST (jaise ₹10,000 ke payment par lagbhag ₹236 fee deduct hoti hai aur ₹9,764 aapke bank account mein aate hain).
  • Settlement Cycle: T+1 ka matlab hai Monday ko aaya paisa Tuesday ko aapke bank mein credit hoga.

4. KYC Documents & Onboarding Requirements

📋 Individual Proprietor Ke Liye Zaroori Kagazaat:

1. PAN Card: Business owner ka personal PAN.
2. Aadhaar Card: DigiLocker authentication ke liye.
3. Cancelled Cheque / Bank Statement: Name clearly printed hona chahiye.
4. Live Website Policy URLs: Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, aur Contact Us with physical address (Mandatory for PG approval!).

Humare backend architecture mein humne verified Razorpay integration payment-config.js aur Client Invoicing System implement kiya hua hai. Agar aapko apni site par payment gateway lagwana hai toh humara E-Commerce Development Portal dekhein.

❓ Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQs)

Bina GST certificate ke payment gateway mil sakta hai?

+
Haan! Razorpay aur Cashfree unregistered individual ya sole proprietorship ko bina GST ke monthly limit ke saath activate kar dete hain.

Payment Gateway aur Payment Link mein kya fark hai?

+
Payment link WhatsApp par bhejkar pay karwaya jaata hai, jabki Payment Gateway website ke checkout button ke andar directly integrated hota hai.

International customers se Dollars ($) mein payment kaise lein?

+
Razorpay aur Stripe international credit card payments accept karte hain, jiske liye International Card Activation request submit karni hoti hai.
