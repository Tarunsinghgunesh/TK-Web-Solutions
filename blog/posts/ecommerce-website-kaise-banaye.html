E-Commerce Website Mein Kya Hona Chahiye?

1. Product Catalog

Saare products clearly listed — photo, naam, description, price. Categories mein organize karo — dhundh na easy hona chahiye.

2. WhatsApp Ordering

India mein sabse effective. "Order on WhatsApp" button — customer click kare aur seedha WhatsApp pe order message aaye. Simple, fast, effective.

3. Category Filtering

Agar 20+ products hain — category wise filter zaroori hai. Customer apni category mein seedha jaaye.

4. High Quality Photos

E-commerce mein photo hi product hai. Achhi lighting mein clear photos — multiple angles se. Photo achhi nahi toh sale nahi hoga.

5. Price Clearly Display

Price hide mat karo. Transparent pricing trust badhata hai.

6. Contact Aur Location

Phone, WhatsApp, address, Google Maps — sab clearly visible hona chahiye.

💡 Chirag Handlooms Bharatpur ka demo dekho: tarunsinghgunesh.github.io/chirag-handlooms-e-commerce — exactly aisi e-commerce site aapke liye ban sakti hai!

7. Mobile Friendly Design

80% orders mobile se aate hain — mobile perfect experience zaroori hai.

8. Social Proof

Customer reviews, ratings, testimonials — ye purchase decision mein help karte hain.

E-Commerce Ke Liye Kya Kya Chahiye Hoga?

  • Products ki list — naam, description, price
  • Product photos — clear, good quality
  • Business logo
  • Contact details
  • Business address
