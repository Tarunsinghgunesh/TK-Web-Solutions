Website Nahi Hone Ke 7 Bade Nuksaan

1. Google Pe Invisible Hona

Aaj Google hi naya "word of mouth" hai. Jab koi aapke type ka business dhundh ta hai aur aap wahan nahi hain — wo seedha competitor ke paas jaata hai.

2. 24/7 Business Nahi Hota

Aapki dukaan raat ko band hoti hai. Par website kabhi band nahi hoti. Raat 2 baje bhi koi aapka number, services, aur prices dekh sakta hai aur inquiry kar sakta hai.

3. Trust Kam Lagta Hai

Aaj ke zamaane mein jis business ki website nahi hai, log usse less professional samajhte hain. "Inki website bhi nahi hai" — ye soch kaam bigaadti hai.

4. Competitor Aage Nikal Jaata Hai

Agar aapke competitor ki website hai aur aapki nahi — wo Google pe dikhega, wo trust karega, wo lead lega. Simple.

5. Koi Proof Nahi Hota

Customers chahte hain dekhna — portfolio, testimonials, previous work. Website ke bina ye possible nahi.

6. WhatsApp Alone Enough Nahi

WhatsApp acha hai, par woh professionally present nahi karta. Website ek permanent, searchable, professional presence hai.

7. Long-term Mein Bada Nuksaan

Jaise jaise digital India badh raha hai, bina website ke businesses peeche reh jaate hain. Abhi ki der baad ki nahi.
