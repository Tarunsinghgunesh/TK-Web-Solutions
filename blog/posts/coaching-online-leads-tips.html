Problem: Students Kyu Nahi Aa Rahe?

Bahut coaching institutes achha padhate hain — experienced teachers hain, results bhi achhe hain. Par fir bhi new student inquiries kam hain. Reason? Digital presence weak hai.

Aaj ka student aur unke parents pehle Google pe research karte hain, phir Instagram dekh te hain, phir WhatsApp pe poochh te hain — aur tab aate hain. Agar in teeno jagah aap strong nahi ho toh inquiries nahi aayengi.

Tip 1 — Professional Website Banwao

Website aapka 24/7 salesman hai. Jab aap so rahe ho, jab institute band hai — tab bhi website kaam karti rehti hai.

Coaching website mein ye hona chahiye:

  • Courses aur batches ki complete list
  • Fee structure (transparent hona chahiye)
  • Faculty introduction
  • Results aur toppers
  • Online inquiry/admission form
  • WhatsApp button

Tip 2 — Google My Business Setup Karo (Free)

Google My Business bilkul free hai aur isse aap Google Maps pe dikh te ho. Jab koi "coaching near me" search kare, aapka naam aaye.

Kaise setup karein:

  1. business.google.com pe jaao
  2. Apna business naam search karo
  3. Address, phone, timing add karo
  4. Photos add karo
  5. Reviews collect karo students se

Tip 3 — WhatsApp Business Use Karo

Normal WhatsApp nahi — WhatsApp Business use karo. Isme catalogue, auto-reply, business profile sab milta hai.

  • Business name aur profile set karo
  • Auto-reply message set karo — "Namaste! Hum jald reply karenge"
  • Broadcast list banao — existing students ko updates bhejo
  • Status mein results, new batches post karo

Tip 4 — Instagram Pe Active Raho

Students aur parents Instagram use karte hain. Ek active Instagram page se free mein reach milti hai.

  • Har hafte 3-4 posts — tips, results, motivational quotes
  • Reels banao — short educational content
  • Stories pe batch updates share karo
  • Location tag karo — Bharatpur wale discover karein

Tip 5 — Student Reviews Collect Karo

Social proof sabse powerful marketing tool hai. Existing students se Google Review aur WhatsApp testimonial lo.

✅ Ek genuine review 10 ads se zyada effective hota hai. Students khushi se dete hain — sirf poochhna hota hai.
