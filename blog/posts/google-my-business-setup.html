Google My Business — Kya Hai Ye?

Google My Business (GMB) — ab iska naam Google Business Profile ho gaya hai — ek free tool hai jo aapke business ko Google Search aur Google Maps pe dikh ata hai.

Jab koi "coaching near me", "E-Mitra Bharatpur", ya "best restaurant Bharatpur" search karta hai — jo 3 local businesses pehle dikh te hain, wo sab GMB use karte hain.

✅ Bilkul free hai. Sirf 15-20 minute lagenge setup mein. Aur results lifelong milte hain.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Step 1 — business.google.com Pe Jaao

Google account se login karo aur business.google.com kholo. "Manage now" ya "Add your business" pe click karo.

Step 2 — Business Name Daalo

Apna exact business name daalo. Agar already exist karta hai toh "Claim this business" option aayega.

Step 3 — Category Select Karo

Sahi category bahut important hai. Examples:

  • Coaching: "Tutoring Service" ya "Educational Institution"
  • E-Mitra: "Government Office" ya "Financial Services"
  • Restaurant: "Restaurant" ya "Indian Restaurant"
  • Shop: Apni specific category

Step 4 — Address Daalo

Exact address daalo — mohalla, road, pin code sab. Google Maps pe exact location set karo drag karke.

Step 5 — Phone Number

Primary phone number daalo — wo number jo actual mein koi use karta ho.

Step 6 — Website Add Karo

Agar website hai toh URL daalo. Agar nahi hai toh pehle website banwao — isse GMB ranking aur better hoti hai.

Step 7 — Timing Set Karo

Exact opening aur closing time daalo — har din ke liye. Sunday band hai toh mark karo.

Step 8 — Verification

Google ek postcard ya phone call se verify karta hai. Postcard aane mein 5-7 din lagte hain — us par ek code hota hai jo enter karna hota hai.

Profile Ko Complete Banao — Extra Tips

  • Photos add karo — shop/office ki, products ki, logo. Zyada photos = better ranking
  • Description likhao — aap kya karte ho, kahan ho, kya special hai
  • Reviews manage karo — customers se reviews maango, har review ka reply do
  • Posts likho — offers, updates, events — Google Posts feature use karo
  • Q&A section — common questions ke jawab pehle se daalo

💡 Pro Tip: Website + GMB combination sabse powerful hai. Website se aapko Google search mein aur GMB se Maps mein ranking milti hai. Dono ek saath use karo.

GMB Ranking Improve Karne Ke Tips

  • Complete profile — har field bharo
  • Regular posts — hafte mein ek post
  • Zyada reviews — 10+ reviews honge toh top 3 mein aane ke chances badh te hain
  • Photos update karte raho
  • Questions ka jawab do

🌐 Website + Google = Maximum Visibility

Website banwao aur GMB setup karo — dono milke double impact karte hain!
