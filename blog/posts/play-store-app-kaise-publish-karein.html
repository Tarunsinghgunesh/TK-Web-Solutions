Play Store App Publish Karne Ki Process

Step 1 — Google Play Developer Account

Google Play Console pe developer account banana hota hai. One-time fee: $25 (approximately ₹2,100). Ye client ko apne naam se banana hota hai — TK Web Solutions ye process mein guide karta hai.

Step 2 — App Development

TK Web Solutions Android Studio mein aapki app develop karta hai. Requirements ke hisaab se — features, design, content sab discuss hota hai pehle.

Step 3 — App Review Ready Karna

Play Store ke liye kuch specific requirements hain:

  • App icon — 512x512 pixels, high quality
  • Feature graphic — 1024x500 pixels
  • Screenshots — minimum 2, different screen sizes
  • App description — keyword rich, clear
  • Privacy Policy URL — zaroori hai
  • Content rating — form fill karna hota hai

Step 4 — Submission

Google Play Console mein sab kuch upload karke submit karo. Google review karta hai — usually 1-7 din lagte hain.

Step 5 — Live!

Approve hone ke baad app Play Store pe live ho jaata hai — search karke, ya direct link se download ho sakta hai.
