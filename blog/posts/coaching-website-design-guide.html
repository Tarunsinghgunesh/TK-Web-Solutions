Coaching Website Kyun Alag Hoti Hai?

Har coaching institute ki website alag hoti hai — school se bhi alag, business se bhi alag. Coaching website ka focus hota hai students aur parents ko convince karna ki ye institute unke liye best choice hai.

Ek achhi coaching website mein kuch specific cheezein honi chahiye jo ye kaam karein effectively.

Coaching Website Mein Ye 10 Cheezein Zaroor Honी Chahiye

1. Hero Section — First Impression

Website ka pehla screen — isme clearly hona chahiye: kaunsa institute hai, kya padhata hai (IIT-JEE/NEET/etc.), aur contact kaise karein. Visitor ke 5 second ke andar usse pata lag jaana chahiye.

2. Courses & Batches List

Saare courses clearly listed honے chahiye — class 9th se 12th, JEE, NEET, NDA, foundation, ya jo bhi ho. Batch timing aur seats available bhi dalo.

3. Fee Structure

Transparent fee structure parents ka trust badhata hai. Hidden charges na rakho — seedha batao kya kya include hai.

4. Results & Achievements

Toppers ki photos aur ranks, board results, selection list — ye sab sabse powerful social proof hai. Jaisa result, vaisa admission.

💡 Pro Tip: Results section mein real students ke names aur photos (permission se) daalo. Ye trust 10x badh aata hai.

5. Faculty Introduction

Teachers ki qualification, experience, aur photo. Parents jaanna chahte hain ki koi kaun padhayega.

6. Online Inquiry/Admission Form

Simple form — naam, class, phone. Leads directly aapke paas aati hain. Bina form ke website incomplete hai.

7. Gallery

Classroom photos, events, awards, activities. Isse institute ki life dikh ti hai.

8. Testimonials

Students aur parents ke quotes — real feedback. Video testimonials aur bhi powerful hain.

9. WhatsApp Button

Prominent WhatsApp button — sirf ek click mein contact. Mobile users ke liye sabse easy.

10. Google Maps

Exact location — new parents dhundh ke aa sakein easily.

Design Tips — Coaching Website Ke Liye

  • Professional colors — dark blue, maroon, green — education feel
  • Clear fonts — padh ne mein easy
  • Mobile first — 70%+ visitors mobile se aate hain
  • Fast loading — 3 second se zyada wait nahi karte log
