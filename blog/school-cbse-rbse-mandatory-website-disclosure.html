School Website Mandatory Public Disclosure Rules 2026: CBSE & RBSE Norms

📅 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 9 min read

⚡ Quick Takeaway / Summary

Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) aur State Boards (RBSE) ne har affiliated private aur government school ke liye official website par "Mandatory Public Disclosure" page banana legally anivarya (mandatory) kar diya hai. Aisa na karne par affiliation renew nahi hota aur heavy penalty lagti hai.

1. School Website Affiliation Rules Ka Kanooni Background

CBSE Circular No. 03/2021 aur latest affiliation bylaws ke anusar, transparency maintain karne ke liye har school ke home page par ek prominent button hona chahiye: "Mandatory Public Disclosure". Yeh page Bina login ke aam janta, parents aur inspection officers ke liye 24x7 accessible hona chahiye.

Rajasthan Board (RBSE) aur ICSE boards ne bhi inspection ke dauran website review ko compulsory audit parameter bana diya hai.

2. 10 Mandatory Documents Jo Website Par Live Hone Chahiye

Document Name Required Format Board Requirement
Affiliation / Extension Letter Official PDF copy signed by Board CBSE / RBSE Mandatory
Society / Trust Registration Certificate Valid Non-Proprietary Character PDF Mandatory
No Objection Certificate (NOC) State Education Department NOC PDF Mandatory
Recognition Certificate (RTE Act 2009) DEO / District Education Officer Order Mandatory
Building Safety Certificate PWD / Government Approved Engineer Certificate Mandatory (Annual Renewal)
Fire Safety Certificate Chief Fire Officer (CFO) Certificate Mandatory (Valid Date)
Water & Sanitation Certificate PHED / Health Department Lab Report Mandatory
Fee Structure of the School Class-wise complete fee breakdown table Mandatory (No hidden fee)
Annual Academic Calendar List of holidays, exams & event schedule Mandatory
School Management Committee (SMC) List of members with designation & contact Mandatory

3. Staff, Teacher Qualifications & Salary Disclosure Format

Board inspection ke samay website par Principal aur teaching staff ka complete biodata check hota hai: Name, Designation, Qualification (B.Ed, M.Sc, etc.), aur Total Experience. Isse parents ka trust 3 guna badh jaata hai.

4. School Website Design Checklist For Best Experience

Sirf documents daalna kaafi nahi hai. Ek modern school website se naye admissions attract hote hain:

  • Hero section mein school campus, sports grounds aur smart classrooms ki real high-resolution photos hon.
  • Toppers corner aur Board Exam results ka photo gallery ho.
  • Online admission enquiry form ho jisse session shuru hote hi leads milein. Dekhein humara School Website Portal Solution aur School Admissions Growth Guide.
