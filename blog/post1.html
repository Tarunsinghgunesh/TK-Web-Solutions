Kya aap jaante hain ki 78% students coaching join karne se pehle online research karte hain? Woh Google pe search karte hain, website dekhte hain, reviews padhte hain — aur tab decide karte hain. Agar aapke coaching ki website nahi hai, toh aap in students ko competitor ke paas bhej rahe hain.

Bharatpur mein coaching competition tezi se badh rahi hai. JEE, NEET, NDA, Board — har subject mein naye coaching centers aa rahe hain. Ek professional website sirf "dikhane ke liye" nahi — yeh aapka 24/7 kaam karne wala salesman hai jo kabhi nahi thakta.

Coaching Website Mein Kya Kya Hona Chahiye?

1. Clear Homepage — Pehla Impression

Pehle 5 second mein visitor ko samajh aana chahiye ki aap kya offer karte hain. Ek strong headline — "Bharatpur Ka Best IIT-JEE Coaching" — aur directly neeche inquiry button. Visitor ka attention bahut kam time ke liye hota hai — use waste mat karo.

2. Courses aur Batches Section

Clearly likho — kaunse subjects, kaunsi classes (9th-12th, Dropper), morning ya evening batch, fees, aur seats. Jab parent ko sab information pehle se mil jaaye — woh directly inquiry karta hai, time waste nahi hota.

3. Faculty Section

Parents jaanna chahte hain ki unka bacha kiski guidance mein padhega. Teachers ki photos, qualifications, experience — yeh trust badhata hai. Ek experienced teacher ka profile professionally present karna conversion mein bahut fark karta hai.

4. Results aur Toppers Page

Yeh aapka sabse powerful marketing tool hai. Students ki achievements — IIT rank, NEET score, Board toppers — photos aur marks ke saath. Real results real trust create karte hain. Ek topper ki photo hazaro pamphlets se zyada kaam karti hai.

5. Online Inquiry Form

Ek simple form — naam, phone, class, kaunsa course — aur submit. Form submit hone pe directly WhatsApp pe notification. Raat ke 11 baje bhi koi inquiry aaye — subah aap follow karo. Bina website ke yeh possible nahi.

6. Photo Gallery

Classroom, annual function, sports day, award ceremony — sab dikhao. Real photos real trust banate hain. Parents dekhte hain ki environment kaisa hai, students kitne engaged rehte hain.

7. WhatsApp Button + Google Maps

Har page pe floating WhatsApp button aur footer mein Google Maps embed. Koi bhi visitor ek click mein contact kar sake — aur location dhundhne mein problem na ho.

Coaching Website Ka Design

Professional design ka matlab complicated nahi hota. Simple, clean, fast loading — jisme mobile pe bhi sab sahi dikhe. TK Web Solutions ki coaching websites mein navy blue aur gold color scheme use hoti hai jo trust aur authority dono communicate karta hai.

Common Galtiyan

  • Cheap ₹2,000 wali website: Broken mobile design, koi SEO nahi, koi support nahi
  • Sirf Facebook page: Algorithm change se reach zero — aapka koi control nahi
  • No contact form: Sirf phone number — raat ko koi inquire nahi kar sakta
  • Outdated content: 2024 ke results 2026 mein — credibility khatam

SEO — Google Pe Rank Kaise Karein

  • Google My Business complete banao — category "Coaching Center" set karo
  • Website title mein city name — "IIT JEE Coaching Bharatpur"
  • Students/parents se Google reviews maango
  • Blog posts likho — "JEE preparation tips", "NEET study plan"
