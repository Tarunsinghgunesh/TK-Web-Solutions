Google My Business Hai Kya Exactly?

GMB Google ka free tool hai jisse aapka business Google Search aur Google Maps pe professionally dikh sakta hai — business name, photos, timing, phone number, reviews sab ke saath. Koi bhi customer aapko Google pe search kare, toh seedha aapki details dikhe. Aur yeh bilkul free hai.

Step-by-Step GMB Setup Guide

Step 1: Account Banana

business.google.com pe jaao. Apne Gmail se login karo. "Add your business to Google" click karo. Business ka exact naam daalo — exactly waisa jaise Google pe dikhana chahte ho.

Step 2: Category aur Location

Business category select karo — yeh bahut important hai. "Coaching center", "Grocery store", "Plumber" — jo aapka actual kaam ho. Phir physical address daalo. Agar ghar se kaam karte ho toh "service area" option use karo.

Step 3: Phone Number aur Website

WhatsApp number daalo — customers directly call ya message kar sakein. Agar website hai toh add karo. Nahi hai? TK Web Solutions se banwao — website + GMB combo sabse powerful combination hai.

Step 4: Verification

Google ek postcard bhejta hai aapke address pe — 5-7 din mein. Usme ek code hota hai. Woh code enter karo aur listing verify ho jaati hai. Kuch cases mein phone ya email verification bhi available hoti hai.

GMB Ko Powerful Banao — Pro Tips

  • Photos daalo: Shop ki, products ki, team ki — minimum 10 photos. Photos wali listings 35% zyada clicks milti hain.
  • Timing sahi rakho: Wrong timing se customers frustrated ho jaate hain aur negative review dete hain.
  • Reviews maango: Happy customers se review maangna aata hai — politely. 4.5+ rating = credibility.
  • Posts daalo: Weekly ek GMB post — offer, update, tip. Yeh ranking mein help karta hai.
  • Q&A section bharо: Common questions khud hi add karo aur jawab do.

Meri personal advice — GMB setup karo aaj hi. Yeh ek kaam jo free hai aur iska ROI sabse fast hai. Aur agar website nahi hai, toh website banwao saath mein — combo mein Google pe dominance aati hai. Aur digital marketing tips ke liye blog dekhte raho.
