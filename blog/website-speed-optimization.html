Website Slow Kyun Hoti Hai?

Most common reasons — jo mujhe har doosri website mein milte hain:

  • Uncompressed, heavy images (JPEG 5MB ki images — common mistake)
  • Too many plugins (WordPress pe)
  • No caching setup
  • Cheap slow hosting
  • Unminified CSS/JavaScript
  • No CDN (Content Delivery Network)

5 Easiest Speed Fixes — Koi Bhi Kar Sakta Hai

Fix 1: Images Compress Karo

Image optimization akela 50-70% speed improve kar deta hai. TinyPNG.com pe free mein compress karo. WebP format use karo JPEG ki jagah — 30% smaller. Every image pe loading="lazy" attribute add karo.

Fix 2: Hosting Upgrade Karo

₹99/month ki hosting aur ₹500/month ki hosting mein speed literally double ka fark hota hai. Hostinger Business ya SiteGround consider karo. Cheap hosting = slow site = lost customers.

Fix 3: Caching Enable Karo

WordPress pe W3 Total Cache ya WP Super Cache free mein available hai. Ek click setup. Repeat visitors ke liye load time 80% tak reduce hota hai.

Fix 4: Unnecessary Plugins Hatao

Har extra plugin load time badhata hai. Audit karo — jo plugins actually use nahi ho rahe, delete karo. 10-15 plugins maximum. Zyada = slow.

Fix 5: Google PageSpeed Insights Use Karo

pagespeed.web.dev pe apna URL daalo — free detailed report milega ki exactly kya fix karna hai. Score 90+ target karo mobile pe.

Speed Ka SEO Se Direct Connection

Google 2021 se "Core Web Vitals" ranking factor hai — jisme page speed central role hai. Fast website = better ranking = more traffic = more sales. Yeh chain directly your business pe impact karti hai.
