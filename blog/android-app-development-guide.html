Android App Kaise Banwaayein — Complete Guide 2026

📅 Feb 23, 2026 By Tarun Singh ⏱ 8 min read

India mein 2026 mein 95% smartphones Android hain. Aapke customers ke haath mein Android hai, unke bachhon ke haath mein Android hai. Ek Android app aapko directly unke phone ki home screen pe jagah deta hai — permanent, always visible. Lekin app development expensive lagti hai. Main Tarun Singh, TK Web Solutions — Bharatpur se ek actual Android developer — honestly bata raha hoon ki Android app development kya involve karta hai aur affordable kaise hota hai.

Kab Android App Banana Chahiye?

Sabke liye app zaroori nahi. Pehle yeh check karo:

  • Daily active users hain ya honge? (Coaching, food delivery, service booking)
  • Push notifications ki zaroorat hai?
  • Offline functionality chahiye?
  • Website already hai aur grow kar rahi hai?
  • Budget minimum ₹10,000 available hai?

Agar 3+ "haan" hain — app banana consider karo. Warna pehle website pe focus karo.

Android App Development Ka Process

Step 1: Requirements Define Karo

App mein kya features chahiye — list banao. Login system, notifications, payments, content delivery — sab specify karo. Requirements clear hone se cost estimate accurate hota hai.

Step 2: Design (UI/UX)

App screens ka design — navigation, layout, color scheme. Good design = better user retention. TK Web Solutions modern Material Design follow karta hai.

Step 3: Development

Backend + Android app code. Timeline depends on complexity. Basic app — 2-3 hafte. Complex app — 1-2 months.

Step 4: Testing

Different Android versions pe test karna zaroori hai — Android 8 se 14 tak. Bug fixes. Performance optimization.

Step 5: Play Store Publish

₹1,750 one-time Play Store developer account fee. Store listing — description, screenshots, icon. Review process 1-7 din. Detailed guide yahan hai.

Cost Breakdown — Honest Estimate

  • Simple app (5-8 screens, basic features): ₹10,000-20,000
  • Medium app (login, content, notifications): ₹20,000-40,000
  • Complex app (payments, real-time features): ₹40,000+
  • Play Store fee (one-time): ₹1,750
