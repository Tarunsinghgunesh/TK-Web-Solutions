Local Developer Se Banwane Ke Fayde

Kuch log directly online freelancers se banwate hain — hota hai, lekin problems bhi hoti hain. Local developer ke advantages clear hain:

  • Face-to-face meeting: Requirements seedhi baat kar ke clear hoti hain
  • Local knowledge: Bharatpur ke market aur customers ko samajhna
  • After-support: Problem aane pe directly mil sakte ho
  • Hindi mein communication: Koi language barrier nahi
  • Faster delivery: Timezone difference nahi hoti

Bharatpur Mein Website Ki Kitni Cost Hogi?

Basic Business Website

5-7 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact, Gallery) — ₹5,000 – ₹12,000. Mobile-friendly, basic SEO, contact form ke saath. Small shops, service providers, professionals ke liye ideal.

Coaching / School Website

Admission form, fee structure, results section, gallery — ₹8,000 – ₹20,000. Lead capture forms aur WhatsApp integration ke saath.

E-Commerce Website

Products, cart, payment gateway — ₹15,000 – ₹40,000. Razorpay/Paytm integration ke saath complete online store.

Android App

Basic app — ₹10,000 – ₹25,000. Play Store publish karne tak complete support.

Kya Check Karo Developer Select Karte Waqt?

Portfolio dekho — real live websites. Client reviews ya references maango. Delivery timeline confirm karo. After-support kya milega yeh puchho. Aur most importantly — kya woh SEO-ready website banata hai ya sirf design?

Main personally har client ke saath transparent rehta hoon — TK Web Solutions mein koi hidden charges nahi, koi fake promises nahi. Website launch ke baad bhi support milta hai. Digital growth ke liye hamare free guides zaroor padho aur seedha TK Web Solutions pe aao consultation ke liye.
