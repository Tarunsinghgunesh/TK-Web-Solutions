Who We Are

TK Web Solutions is a professional web and Android app development agency based in Bharatpur, Rajasthan, India. We were founded in 2023 with one clear mission — to bring affordable, high-quality digital solutions to local businesses, educational institutions, and entrepreneurs across India.

We understand that every business is unique. That's why we don't offer one-size-fits-all solutions. Instead, we take time to understand your specific needs, goals, and target audience — then we craft a digital solution that truly works for you.

Hi! I'm Tarun Singh — a self-taught web developer and Android app developer from Bharatpur, Rajasthan. I am the sole founder and operator of TK Web Solutions.

My journey into web development started around 2022–2023. Like many self-starters, I began with online tutorials, practice projects, and a lot of trial and error. I spent countless hours learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and eventually Android app development using Android Studio.

What really motivated me was seeing local businesses around me — coaching centers, schools, small shops, and service providers — struggling to reach customers online. Most of them had no website, no digital presence, and no idea how to get started. I realized I could bridge that gap.

"Every local business deserves a strong online presence — regardless of their size or budget."

That belief became the foundation of TK Web Solutions. Since 2023, I have built and delivered websites and apps for coaching institutes, schools, local businesses, NGOs, and e-mitra centers in Bharatpur and beyond.

I handle every aspect of each project personally — from initial consultation and design, to development, testing, deployment, and after-delivery support. This ensures consistent quality and clear communication with every client.
